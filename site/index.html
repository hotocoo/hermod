Spaces that belong to the people in them.

Hermod is a desktop app where every install is a small peer in an encrypted mesh. Chat, share and plan with friends. No central server, no accounts, no telemetry.

Download Hermod View source

How it works

Each space has its own key. Your messages and files are encrypted on your device and synced straight to the other members. Peers that help carry traffic cannot read it.

Invite someone with a link. The code stays in the part of the link your browser never sends anywhere, and opens right in the app.

  • Private by design. Spaces are encrypted with keys only members hold.
  • No server to trust. Members' devices sync with each other.
  • You choose what to share. Relaying can be capped or switched off.
  • Open source. Apache-2.0, read every line.

Honest limits

  • Two people behind strict home routers may fail to connect until a reachable Hermod peer is online.
  • Public IPFS and STUN servers are third-party services Hermod uses to find peers.
  • Members cannot be removed and invite codes cannot be revoked yet.
